How to recycle clothes

Clothes in good condition can be donated to charity shops or clothing banks. Many high street stores now offer take-back schemes. Worn-out textiles can go to textile recycling bins — never regular landfill.

🌱

Understanding material labels

Clothing labels list materials by percentage. The higher a material appears on the list, the greater its share. MADE-BY grades fibres from Class A (most sustainable) to Class E (least) based on environmental impact.

💧

Wash smarter

Washing clothes at 30°C uses up to 40% less energy than 40°C. Use a full load, avoid tumble drying, and wash synthetic fabrics in a microplastic-catching bag to reduce ocean pollution.

🛍️

Buy less, buy better

The most sustainable garment is one that already exists. Consider secondhand shopping, clothing swaps, and renting for occasions. When buying new, choose quality over quantity.
